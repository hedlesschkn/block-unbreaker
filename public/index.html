BLOCKUNBREAKER

Block breaker, played from the wrong side.

You never touch the paddle. You never touch the ball.

You place the blocks — and the paddle is not on your side.

It returns almost everything. So don't build a wall to stop the ball: build a machine that aims it, into the gutters it cannot reach.

Multiplayer arrives in Phase 3. Single player is the whole game for now.
